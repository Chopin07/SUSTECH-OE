• Scalar & Vector (Quantity) 标量&⽮量
  Scalar refers to a quantity whose value (值) could be represented
  by a real number (positive or negative).
  Examples: Temperature 温度; Length ⻓度; Weight 重量
  A vector quantity has both a magnitude (量、⼤⼩、模) and a
  direction (⽅向) in space (空间) .
  Examples: Force ⼒; Speed 速度


  Note:
  1. Always give a second thought on the terms in red.
  2. Most physical quantities (scalar or vector) have their own units (单位)；
  3. Space has dimensions (维度，2-D, 3-D, …n-D);
